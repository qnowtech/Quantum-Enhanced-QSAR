    Is the difference only the device?

    No. Noise alone does not reproduce the crossing.

    Noise crosses about 39% of the six QPU-only molecules, and 12% of controls far from the boundary. 8d and XMD14-124 cross only on the device.

Globant + haiqu + + Qnow

Same molecules, different lists

  1. 1

    The two representations propose different compounds for assay.

    Only the measured vector assays 8d and XMD14-124, plus four inactives. The table would assay six actives the vector never would. Noise does not cause the split.

  2. 2

    A higher ranking is not a better prediction.

    The table reaches 0.879 against 0.826, without conventional significance (DeLong, p = 0.117). What moves to assay is the label each model assigns.

  3. 3

    Assay the disagreement. Do not merge it away.

    Trust the compounds both models call active. Spend wells on what only the vector sees, and on what only the table sees. A quantum QSAR earns its place there: chemistry the table never proposes. One higher score erases it.

Outside this work's scope: clinical benefit, a general quantum advantage in drug discovery, a dossier-grade QSAR model. Neither recovered structure is an approved drug.
